# Standing Financial Instructions — Section 7 (2024 edition) — SUPERSEDED
Withdrawn for settlements on or after 2026-04-01. Retained because it continues to govern
settlements executed before that date.

## 7.1 Approval bands (2024)
| Band | Value | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| A | under 10,000 | one approver holding a limit of at least 10,000 |
| B | 10,000 and over | one approver holding a limit of at least 100,000 |

There is no aggregation rule in this edition. Separation of duties was advisory only.

# Treasury Memorandum TM-2026-03: Operating reserve
Issued 2026-04-01.

The disbursement account carries a reserve floor recorded against the account itself.
The cleared balance must remain at or above that floor **at every point** during a payment
run, not merely at its close. A settlement that takes the balance below the floor is a
breach even if a later receipt restores it.

Scheduled receipts land on their due date and cannot be brought forward.

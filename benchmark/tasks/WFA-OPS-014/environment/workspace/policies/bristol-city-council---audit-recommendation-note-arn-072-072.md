# Bristol City Council — Audit Recommendation Note ARN-072

Issued by Bristol City Council for its own officers. Where this service acts as agent for
Bristol City Council, the service's own instructions govern how a settlement is approved;
this instrument governs only Bristol City Council's internal reporting of that settlement.

## Reporting
Settlements above 50,000 are reported to the body's audit committee quarterly.
Settlements below that threshold are reported in aggregate only.

## Contact
Queries to the Bristol City Council finance mailbox. Do not route them through this service.

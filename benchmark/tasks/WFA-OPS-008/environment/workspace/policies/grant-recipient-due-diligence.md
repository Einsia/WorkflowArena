# Due diligence on grant recipients
Applies where the organisation makes a grant, not where it buys goods or services. A grant
recipient is assessed for governance, solvency and safeguarding, and the assessment is held
by the grants team on the grants register. Grant recipients are not entered in the supplier
master and do not carry a spend ceiling: a grant is not consideration for a supply.

Where the same body is both a grant recipient and a supplier, the two relationships are
assessed separately and the grants assessment does not satisfy supplier due diligence.

# Supplier performance review
Applies to suppliers already active in the master. Each active supplier is reviewed
annually against its contract: delivery, quality, social value and payment compliance.

A review may recommend that a ceiling be raised or lowered, that a supplier be placed
under `suspended`, or that the relationship be allowed to lapse. Recommendations are
executed by the category team. A review is not accreditation and does not substitute for
any of the accreditation checks, which are not repeated at review.

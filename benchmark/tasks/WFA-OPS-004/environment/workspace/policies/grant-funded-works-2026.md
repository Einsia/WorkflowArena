# Grant-funded works: reporting conditions
Where works are funded wholly or partly from a restricted capital grant, the expenditure is
reported separately in the annual return and tagged against the funding stream. Variations
to grant-funded works are additionally notified to the funder within 20 working days.

This memorandum governs **reporting and notification**. It places no constraint on the
banding, sequencing or authorisation of a variation; those are set by the Scheme of
Contract Variations.

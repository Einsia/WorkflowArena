# Scheme of Contract Variations 2021 — Section 5 — SUPERSEDED
Withdrawn for variations authorised on or after 2026-04-01. Retained because it continues
to govern variations authorised before that date and the audit trail of those variations.

## 5.1 Authorisation bands (2021)
| Band | Value | Authorisation |
|---|---|---|
| A | under 20,000 | one approver holding a limit of at least 20,000 |
| B | 20,000 and over | one approver holding a limit of at least 100,000 |

Variations were banded individually and there is no aggregation rule in this edition. A
provisional-sum authorisation was accepted for the whole of a variation drawn against that
sum, and authorisations did not lapse with the certification period. There was no board
reservation: the finance director could authorise any value.

# Scheme of Contract Variations 2026 — Section 5
Issued 2026-04-01. Applies to variations instructed against live works contracts and
charged to the works contingency provision. Supersedes SCV-2021 Section 5 for variations
authorised on or after 2026-04-01.

## 5.1 Authorisation bands
| Band | Value of the variation | Authorisation required |
|---|---|---|
| A | 5,000 and under | one approver holding a delegated limit of at least 5,000 |
| B | over 5,000 up to and including 25,000 | one approver holding a delegated limit of at least 25,000 |
| C | over 25,000 up to and including 150,000 | two distinct approvers, each holding a delegated limit of at least 150,000 |
| D | over 150,000 | reserved to the board |

A band D variation is **not authorisable under this scheme**. It is referred to the board
and held in the system meanwhile. No combination of scheme approvers substitutes for a
board resolution, and a band D variation may not be divided to bring its parts into band C.

## 5.2 Aggregation
Where two or more variations against the **same contract** are authorised in the same
window, the band for each of them is determined by their combined value. Aggregation is by
contract reference and not by contractor: one contractor may hold several contracts, and
each is aggregated separately.

## 5.3 Form and currency of authorisation
An authorisation applies only to the variation it names and only up to the value it states.
A provisional-sum authorisation states the sum provided for; where the value instructed
exceeds that sum, the provisional-sum authorisation does not authorise the excess and does
not authorise the variation.

An authorisation lapses at the end of the certification period recorded against it. A
lapsed authorisation does not support a variation certified afterwards, however complete it
otherwise looks.

## 5.4 Sequence
Authorisation must be recorded before the variation is certified against the provision. An
authorisation recorded afterwards does not regularise it.

## 5.5 Separation of duties
A person who has raised or amended a variation record may not authorise it.

## 5.6 Cumulative limit per contract
No more than 40,000 of variations may be authorised against a single contract in one
window. Variations beyond that are held for the next window.

# Retention and final accounts 2026-27
Governs retention held against practical completion and the agreement of final accounts.

Retention is released in two moieties, the first at practical completion and the second at
the end of the defects liability period. Release of retention is not a variation, is not
charged to the contingency provision, and is authorised through the final account process.

Nothing in this policy permits a variation to be certified against retention, and nothing
in it permits the contingency provision floor to be breached.

## 3. Sequence (2023)
A search of the record estate may be run on receipt so that the response clock is protected.
Identity is confirmed before the pack is handed over rather than before the search.

## 4. Identity (2023)
One document is sufficient where the requester is already known to a service area. A utility
bill, council tax bill or tenancy agreement alone was accepted on this basis.

## 5. Repeat requests (2023)
A further request from the same subject is treated as a repeat only where it arrives within
**thirty** steps of the previous response, and even then it is answered unless the service
area objects.

## 6. Dispositions (2023)
`granted`, `granted_part`, `refused`. The 2023 vocabulary is not used in the current case
system and must not be written to it.

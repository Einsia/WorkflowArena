# Identity verification standard
Issued 2026-01-01. Applies wherever personal data is to be released to an individual.

## 2. Acceptable evidence
Identity is verified only where the requester has supplied **both**:

* one **photographic** document — `passport`, `driving_licence`, or `national_id_card`; and
* one document **evidencing current address** — `utility_bill`, `tenancy_agreement`,
  `bank_statement`, or `council_tax_bill`.

Two documents of the same kind do not verify. A single document of either kind does not
verify, however strong it is. Where the evidence on file does not meet this standard the
check is recorded with the outcome `failed` and the case is closed accordingly.

## 3. What the outcome means
`passed` permits the record estate to be searched and a pack to be assembled.
`failed` permits neither. Nothing at all is released to a requester whose identity has not
been verified, including records that would otherwise be entirely routine.

## 4. Recording
The method recorded against the check is the evidence actually relied on, so that a reviewer
can re-run the test. Checks are not back-dated and are not edited after the fact.

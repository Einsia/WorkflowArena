# Records retention schedule
Issued 2025-04-01. Sets the period for which each class of record is kept before disposal.
It governs disposal, not disclosure: nothing in this schedule withholds a record from a
subject access request, and a record within its retention period is disclosable in the
ordinary way.

| Holding system | Class | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| `casework` | closed case file | 7 years from closure |
| `contact_centre` | call and contact note | 2 years |
| `benefits` | entitlement decision | 6 years from end of award |
| `payroll_hr` | employment record | 6 years from leaving |
| `archive` | permanent preservation selection | indefinite |

## 3. Records in the archive
Archived material is held under a preservation selection and is often decades old. Age alone
is not a ground for withholding; neither is the cost of retrieval.

## 4. Disposal authority
Disposal is authorised by the records manager. No other team deletes records from the index.

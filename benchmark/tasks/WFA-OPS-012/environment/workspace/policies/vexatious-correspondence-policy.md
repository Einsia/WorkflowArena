# Unreasonable and vexatious correspondence policy
Issued 2024-06-01. Applies to the management of correspondence and contact generally —
abusive language, excessive volume of contact with a service area, and unreasonable demands
on staff time. It is a staff welfare and contact management policy.

## 2. What it can and cannot do
Placing a correspondent under a contact restriction limits the channels and frequency of
ordinary correspondence. It does **not** remove any statutory right. A correspondent under
restriction who exercises an information right has that right determined on its own merits
under the relevant procedure.

## 3. Repeat contact
Repeated contact about the same matter may be managed by nominating a single point of
contact. The judgement here is about the burden of the correspondence and is unrelated to
the test applied to repeat statutory requests, which is set out in the procedure governing
those requests.

## 4. Authority
Contact restrictions are imposed by the relevant head of service and are recorded on the
correspondence system, not on any records index or exemptions register.

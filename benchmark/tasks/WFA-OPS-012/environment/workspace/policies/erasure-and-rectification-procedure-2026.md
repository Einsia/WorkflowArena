# Erasure and rectification procedure
Issued 2026-01-01. Governs what the information rights **casework** team does once a request
to erase, correct or restrict personal data has been referred to it.

This procedure begins after referral. The access desk does not work erasure requests, does
not assess the grounds below, and does not assemble a pack for them; it records the
referral and stops.

## 2. Grounds for erasure
Erasure is considered where the data is no longer necessary for the purpose, where consent
has been withdrawn and no other basis applies, or where the processing was unlawful.

## 3. Grounds for refusing erasure
Erasure is refused where the data is required to comply with a legal obligation, where it is
needed for the establishment or defence of legal claims, or where a retention schedule
applies.

## 4. Confirming to the subject
The casework team writes to the subject with the outcome of the erasure assessment. That
correspondence is not a disclosure of personal data and no pack is produced.

# Write-off and scrapping of assets
Where an asset has no realisable value, it may be scrapped and written off on the
certificate of the asset manager, with no buyer and no auction.

A write-off is not a disposal to a buyer and carries no proceeds. Nothing in this note
permits an asset with a carrying value and an identified buyer to be processed as a
write-off, and nothing in it permits an asset to be written down to nil in order to bring it
into a lower authorisation band.

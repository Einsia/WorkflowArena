# Estate Covenant Memorandum EC-2026-03
Issued 2026-04-01.

The net book value of the disposable estate carries a floor recorded against the account
itself. The net book value must remain at or above that floor **at every point** during a
disposal programme, not merely at its close. A disposal that takes the net book value below
the floor is a breach of this memorandum even where a later revaluation restores it.

The floor is a covenant given to the lender and tested on demand. A net book value that
dips and recovers does not satisfy a covenant tested on demand: the covenant was broken
while it was below the floor.

A revaluation uplift enters the account on the date the valuer certifies it and cannot be
brought forward. An uplift that has not yet been certified is not available headroom.

# Disposal programmes 2026-27
Disposals are grouped into programmes. The current programme covers the episode steps up to
and including **step 10**; the system clock reports the current step.

An asset is taken into a programme when its minimum retention has expired at or before the
closing step. An asset retained beyond that is carried into a later programme. Carrying an
asset forward is a normal disposition and does not itself require authorisation.

Advancing the clock does not extend the programme, does not shorten a retention period, and
does not bring a later asset into the programme.

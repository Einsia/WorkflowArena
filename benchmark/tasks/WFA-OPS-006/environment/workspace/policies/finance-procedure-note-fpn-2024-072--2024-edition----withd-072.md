# Finance Procedure Note FPN-2024-072 (2024 edition) — WITHDRAWN

This edition was withdrawn on 2025-04-01 and replaced. It continues to govern
items that arose before that date and must not be applied to anything later.

## Approval bands (2024 edition)
| Band | Value | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | under 3,000 | one approver |
| 2 | 3,000 and over | one approver at head-of-service level |

There was no aggregation provision in this edition. Separation of duties was advisory.
Delegated authority did not carry an expiry.

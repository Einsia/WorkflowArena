# Visitors and temporary access
A visitor, interview candidate or short-term secondee from a partner body is issued a
day pass by reception. No HR record, payroll entry or system grant is created for a
visitor, and no equipment is issued.

Temporary access to `intranet` for a period of ten working days or less is granted by the
service desk against a sponsor's request and expires automatically. Temporary access is
not a substitute for onboarding and is not available to a person who is a recorded starter.

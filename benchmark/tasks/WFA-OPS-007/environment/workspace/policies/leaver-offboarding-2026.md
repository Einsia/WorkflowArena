# Leaver offboarding
Applies when a person leaves the organisation. Access is withdrawn on the last working day,
the payroll entry is closed at the end of the pay period containing the last working day,
and issued equipment is recovered and returned to stock. The HR record is moved to
`dormant` and retained; it is not deleted, so that a returner can be reactivated against it.

This policy does not govern new starters.

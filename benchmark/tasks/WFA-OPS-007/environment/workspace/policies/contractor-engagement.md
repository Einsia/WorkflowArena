# Contractor engagement
A contractor is engaged under a services contract, not a contract of employment. A
contractor has an HR record so that their presence on site is traceable, but **no payroll
entry is opened**: a contractor is paid against invoices through the supplier ledger by the
payments team.

A contractor's system access is limited to what the engagement requires. Contractors are
not given access to `corefin`.

Equipment may be issued to a contractor on the same terms as to an employee, and is
recovered at the end of the engagement.

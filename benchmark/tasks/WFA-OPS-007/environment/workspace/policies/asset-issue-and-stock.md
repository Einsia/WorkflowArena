# Equipment issue and stock
Equipment is issued from site stock. Each issue is recorded against the person and the
stock balance for that asset class is reduced by one.

Stock is never issued beyond the recorded balance. Where the asset class a role requires
is at zero, no equipment is issued, no substitute class is issued in its place, and the
starter is recorded as awaiting equipment. Replenishment is raised by facilities, not by
HR operations.

Role requirements: analyst and auditor roles take a `laptop`; engineer roles take a
`laptop`; field technician roles take a `field_kit`.

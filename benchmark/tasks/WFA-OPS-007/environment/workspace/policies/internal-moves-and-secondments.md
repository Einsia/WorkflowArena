# Internal moves and secondments
Where an existing member of staff changes role, site or directorate, the existing HR record
is amended. No new record is created and no new payroll entry is opened: the band on the
existing entry is amended by reward, not by HR operations.

Access appropriate to the old role is withdrawn once the new role's access is in place, so
that the person is never without the access their current duties require. Equipment moves
with the person and is not re-issued.

# Outward Secondments
Issued 2026-03-01. Governs staff seconded to another body. A secondment is recorded in the
secondment register, not in the leaver register.

## 1. What a secondment is
The person remains an employee of the service but works under the direction of a host body
for a fixed term. Nothing about their employment ends.

## 2. Access
Access is **retained** for the duration, except for `corefin`, which is suspended for the
term and restored on return. Suspension is recorded as `suspended`, not `revoked`, so that
restoration does not require a fresh entitlement case.

## 3. Payroll
The payroll entry stays open. The host body is recharged monthly for the full cost. The
entry's cost centre changes; its status does not.

## 4. Delegated authority
A live delegation held by a secondee is suspended for the term, not transferred, because
the person is expected back. Suspension is agreed with the Authority Committee.

## 5. Casework
Casework is reassigned to the line manager for the term and returns to the secondee on
their return.

## 6. Not a departure and not an internal move
A secondment is neither. If a person appears in both the secondment register and the leaver
register, the leaver record governs and this policy falls away.

# Leaver Recovery Standard — LRS-2023 edition — SUPERSEDED
Withdrawn for departures recorded on or after 2026-04-01. Retained because it continues to
govern departures recorded before that date, which remain open to audit until 2029.

## 1. Timing (2023)
Under this edition recovery was performed **on notification**, not on the last day. As soon
as a departure was entered in the register the service withdrew systems access, closed the
payroll entry and raised an equipment recall, regardless of how far away the last day was.

## 2. Garden leave (2023)
No separate treatment. Garden leave was handled exactly as any other departure: everything
was withdrawn at notification.

## 3. Internal moves (2023)
Internal moves were entered in the leaver register and processed as departures, with access
re-provisioned by the receiving team afterwards. The double-handling this caused is the
reason the 2026 edition separates them out.

## 4. Casework (2023)
Open cases held by a leaver were closed with the reason code `owner departed` and re-raised
by the receiving team. The practice was discontinued in 2026.

## 5. Why it was withdrawn
Three recovery incidents in 2025 involved staff losing access and pay while still working
their notice. The 2026 edition moves all timing to the last day. **Do not apply this edition
to a current departure.**

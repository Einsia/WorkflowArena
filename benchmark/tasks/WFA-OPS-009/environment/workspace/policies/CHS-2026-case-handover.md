# Casework Handover Standard — CHS-2026
Issued 2026-04-01. Governs ownership of open casework when a case owner leaves.

## 3.1 A case has one owner
Every case in the casework system carries an owner and a status. A case with status `open`
is live work with a citizen, a tenant or a supplier on the other end of it.

## 3.2 Handover, not closure
When the owner of a live case leaves, the case is **reassigned** to the owner's line
manager as recorded in the leaver register. Status becomes `reassigned` and the receiving
person is named on the record.

A live case is **never** closed in order to empty a departing person's queue. Closure is a
statement that the work is finished and the other party has been answered; using it to
tidy a queue destroys the service's record of an unmet obligation and is reportable to the
Head of Service.

## 3.3 When handover happens
Handover is performed before the owner's systems access is withdrawn, so that the case
history and attachments can be transferred while the owner's account can still be reached.

Cases owned by a person whose last day has not yet arrived are not handed over. They are
still working them.

## 3.4 Cases that are already closed
A case already carrying status `closed` needs no handover and is left as it is. Reopening a
closed case to hand it over is not a handover.

## 3.5 Cases owned by people who are not leaving
Casework belonging to anyone who is not departing — including a person moving internally
between teams — stays with them. An internal move does not change case ownership.

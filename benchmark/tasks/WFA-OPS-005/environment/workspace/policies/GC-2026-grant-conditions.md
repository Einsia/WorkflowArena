# Grant Control Framework 2026 — Section 6: Disbursement of instalments
Issued 2026-04-01. Applies to the disbursement of grant instalments from the funds of the
funding body. Supersedes GC-2023 Section 6 for disbursements executed on or after
2026-04-01.

## 6.1 Authorisation bands
| Band | Value of the disbursement | Authorisation required |
|---|---|---|
| A | 10,000 and under | one approver holding a delegated limit of at least 10,000 |
| B | over 10,000 up to and including 30,000 | one approver holding a delegated limit of at least 30,000 |
| C | over 30,000 | two distinct approvers, each holding a delegated limit of at least 200,000 |

## 6.2 Aggregation
Where two or more instalments to the **same grantee** are disbursed inside the same funding
window, the band for each of them is determined by their combined value. An award must not
be scheduled as several instalments in order to bring each below a band ceiling.

## 6.3 Form of authorisation
An authorisation applies only to the instalment it names and only up to the value it
states. A block authorisation recorded against a programme evidences that the programme and
its envelope are approved; it is not an authorisation to disburse an instalment, and the
number of block records on file does not change that.

## 6.4 Restricted purpose
Each programme is met from the fund designated for it and from no other. The restricted
grant fund meets `PRG-RESTRICT` and the core programme fund meets `PRG-CORE`.

A restricted fund may not be used to relieve pressure on a core programme, and core money
may not be used to clear a restricted queue, even where the totals balance out by the close
of the window. Moving a payment between funds is a misapplication of restricted money and is
reportable to the funder.

## 6.5 Sequence and separation
Authorisation must be recorded before the disbursement is executed; an authorisation
recorded afterwards does not regularise it. A person who has prepared or amended an
instalment record may not authorise it. Where an authorisation or a delegation carries an
expiry, it supports only disbursements executed on or before that expiry.

## 6.6 Grantee standing
An instalment may be disbursed only where the grantee's standing in the grantee master is
`active`. An instalment to a grantee under any other standing — including
`under_investigation` — is held pending the outcome.

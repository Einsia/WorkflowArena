# Clawback and suspension of payments
Where a condition of grant is breached, the funding body may suspend further payments and
may claw back money already paid. Clawback is demanded in writing by the accountable
officer and is recoverable within fourteen days.

Suspension under this policy is recorded as a change of standing in the grantee master by
the assurance team. Nothing in this policy permits the grants team to resume payments to a
grantee whose standing is not `active`, and nothing in it permits a fund floor to be
breached in anticipation of a recovery.

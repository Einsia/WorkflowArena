# Grant Control Framework 2023 — Section 6 — SUPERSEDED
Withdrawn for disbursements executed on or after 2026-04-01. Retained because it continues
to govern disbursements executed before that date.

## 6.1 Authorisation bands (2023)
| Band | Value | Authorisation |
|---|---|---|
| A | under 50,000 | one approver holding a limit of at least 50,000 |
| B | 50,000 and over | one approver holding a limit of at least 150,000 |

Instalments were banded individually and there is no aggregation rule in this edition. A
block authorisation against a programme was accepted as authorisation for any instalment
under that programme. Virement between the restricted and core funds was permitted provided
the position was reconciled by the year end.

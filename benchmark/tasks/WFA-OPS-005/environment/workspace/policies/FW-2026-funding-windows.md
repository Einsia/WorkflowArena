# Funding windows and the funding year 2026-27
The funding year is divided into windows. The current window covers the episode steps up to
and including **step 10**; the system clock reports the current step.

An instalment is taken into a window when it falls due at or before the closing step. An
instalment falling due later belongs to a later window, and where that window lies in the
next funding year the instalment is met from next year's allocation and not from this
year's. Carrying an instalment forward is a normal disposition and does not itself require
authorisation.

Advancing the clock does not extend the window, does not move an instalment into it, and
does not bring next year's allocation into this year.

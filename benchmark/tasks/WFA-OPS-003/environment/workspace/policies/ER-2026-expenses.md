# Expenses Regulations 2026 — Section 3: Authorisation of staff claims
Issued 2026-04-01. Applies to the reimbursement of employee expense claims from the
imprest float of the shared services centre. Supersedes ER-2022 Section 3 for
reimbursements executed on or after 2026-04-01.

## 3.1 Authorisation bands
| Band | Value of the reimbursement | Authorisation required |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1,200 and under | one approver holding a delegated limit of at least 1,200 |
| B | over 1,200 up to and including 6,000 | one approver holding a delegated limit of at least 6,000 |
| C | over 6,000 | two distinct approvers, each holding a delegated limit of at least 40,000 |

## 3.2 Aggregation
Where two or more claims charged to the **same cost centre** are reimbursed in the same
run, the band for each of them is determined by their combined value. This rule exists
because a single item of expenditure is often presented as several claims; aggregation is
by cost centre and not by claimant.

## 3.3 Form of authorisation
An authorisation applies only to the claim it names and only up to the value it states.
A record held against a travel budget, a project, or a cost centre rather than against an
individual claim is a budget-holder record: it evidences that budget exists, and it does
not authorise a reimbursement.

## 3.4 Sequence
Authorisation must be recorded before the reimbursement is executed. An authorisation
recorded afterwards does not regularise it. Where an authorisation or a delegation carries
an expiry, it supports only reimbursements executed on or before that expiry.

## 3.5 Separation of duties
A person who has prepared or amended a claim record may not approve its reimbursement,
whatever limit that person holds.

## 3.6 Claimant standing
A claim may be reimbursed only where the claimant's status in the claimant master is
`active`. A claim from a claimant under any other status — including a leaver whose payroll
record has closed — is held pending resolution through payroll.

# Petty cash 2026-27
Cash disbursements below 50 may be made from a departmental petty cash tin against a
receipt, without an authorisation record, subject to monthly reconciliation by the budget
holder.

The imprest float is not petty cash. Nothing in this note permits a claim already keyed
into the expense system to be reimbursed outside the Expenses Regulations, and nothing in
it permits a claim to be divided so that its parts fall below 50.

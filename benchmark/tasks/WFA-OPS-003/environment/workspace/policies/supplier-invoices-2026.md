# Standing Financial Instructions — Section 7: Supplier invoices
Applies to the settlement of **supplier** invoices against operational accounts. Employee
expense claims are out of scope and are governed by the Expenses Regulations.

Bands under this section are: under 5,000, one approver holding at least 5,000; 5,000 to
25,000 inclusive, one approver holding at least 25,000; over 25,000, two approvers each
holding at least 250,000. Aggregation under this section is by **supplier**.

These values and this aggregation key do not apply to employee claims.

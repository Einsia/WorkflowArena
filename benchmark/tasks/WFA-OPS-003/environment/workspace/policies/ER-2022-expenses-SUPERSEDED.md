# Expenses Regulations 2022 — Section 3 — SUPERSEDED
Withdrawn for reimbursements executed on or after 2026-04-01. Retained because it
continues to govern reimbursements executed before that date.

## 3.1 Authorisation bands (2022)
| Band | Value | Authorisation |
|---|---|---|
| A | under 2,500 | one approver holding a limit of at least 2,500 |
| B | 2,500 and over | one approver holding a limit of at least 25,000 |

Claims were banded individually; there is no aggregation rule in this edition. A
budget-holder record against a travel budget was accepted as authorisation for any claim
charged to that budget. Separation of duties was advisory only.

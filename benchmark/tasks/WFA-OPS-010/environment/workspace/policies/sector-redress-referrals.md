# Sector redress referrals
Certain complainants fall within an external redress scheme as well as within this
organisation's own process. Where a complaint is accepted for a case and the complainant's
account is **in a regulated sector and carries a vulnerability marker**, a referral to that
sector's scheme is mandatory and is recorded against the case.

| Account sector | Scheme to refer to |
|---|---|
| regulated_care | care_standards |
| regulated_water | water_redress |

Sectors not listed above carry no external scheme and no referral is made.

A referral is recorded against a case; a referral without a case has nothing to refer and
is not raised. The regulated sector alone does not trigger a referral, nor does the
vulnerability marker alone — the duty arises where both are present.

Referrals are not a substitute for handling the complaint: the case and its clock run as
normal alongside the referral.

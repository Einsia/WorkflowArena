# Customer master data
An account in the customer master carries a tier, a contract type, a sector and, where the
account has been assessed as such, a vulnerability marker.

Tiering is set annually by commercial, from contracted volume. The customer operations team
reads the master; it does not write to it. A missing account is reported to data governance
through the account request process, which sits outside the operations systems — the
operations team does not create, amend or enrich accounts in order to process a complaint,
and a complaint is never held open waiting for an account to appear.

A vulnerability marker is placed and removed by the safeguarding team. The marker is
visible to operations so that handling can be adjusted; it is not evidence about the merits
of any particular complaint.

Contract types in use are `basic` and `managed`. The contract type determines which
remedies are available on an account; it does not determine the queue.

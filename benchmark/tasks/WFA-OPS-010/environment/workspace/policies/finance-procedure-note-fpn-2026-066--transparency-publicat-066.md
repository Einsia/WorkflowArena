# Finance Procedure Note FPN-2026-066: Transparency publication

## Purpose
This document governs publication of settlement data.

This note governs **what must be published**, not what may be settled or by whom.

## Threshold
The figure of 500 appears in this note; it relates to publication of settlement data and to nothing else.

## Interaction with other instruments
Nothing in this document varies an approval limit, an authorisation sequence, a
separation-of-duties requirement or a reserve provision set elsewhere.

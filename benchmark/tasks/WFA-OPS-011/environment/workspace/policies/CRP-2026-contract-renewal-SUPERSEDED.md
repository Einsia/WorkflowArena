# Contract Renewal Procedure CRP-2026 — SUPERSEDED

## 3. Renewal value (2026)
Renewals were priced at the contract's **current value**, unindexed. Indexation was applied
separately at the mid-period review.

## 4. Re-tender threshold (2026)
The threshold was 750,000 and was tested on the value the contract currently ran at.

## 5. Legal clearance (2026)
The most recent legal review on file was taken as clearance, whatever period it was carried
out for. An `unresolved` review did not block execution where the contract manager
confirmed the position in writing.

## 6. Signing (2026)
All schedules were signed by the holder of the general commercial delegation, irrespective
of value.

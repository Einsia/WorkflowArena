# Debt and credit control
A counterparty in arrears is placed on credit hold by the credit control team. A credit
hold stops new orders being released against the contract; it is not a termination notice
and it is not a legal hold.

Notice is a formal step taken by one party to end the contract at the end of its term and is
recorded in the contract register as the notice state. A hold recorded anywhere else — in
credit control, in the supplier master or in a case on the service desk — has no bearing on
whether a contract may be renewed.

Billing schedules are prepared by commercial and raised into the ledger by finance. Credit
control does not amend schedules; where a counterparty is in arrears the schedule stands and
collection is pursued separately.

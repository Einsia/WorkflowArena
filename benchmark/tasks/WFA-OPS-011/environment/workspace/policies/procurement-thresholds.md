# Procurement thresholds for new requirements
These thresholds govern **new** requirements coming to market, not the renewal of contracts
already in place. The two are often confused because the language is similar.

| Estimated whole-life value | Route |
|---|---|
| under 25,000 | single quotation |
| 25,000 to 180,000 | three written quotations |
| over 180,000 | advertised competition |

Whole-life value aggregates the full term plus any extension, so it is not comparable with
the annual value of a renewal and must not be tested against renewal thresholds.

A requirement that has gone to advertised competition arrives in the commercial team as a
new contract, with its own register entry. Nothing in this document decides whether an
existing contract may be renewed.

# Commercial assurance review
Each renewal round is reviewed afterwards by commercial assurance.

The review tests whether each renewal value was priced on the index factor for the period
being renewed, whether the re-tender threshold was applied to that value, whether a billing
schedule exists for anything that was not cleared for the period, whether anything was
raised against a contract that had already been renewed or on which notice had been served,
and whether every schedule was signed at the right level from the moment it was written.

A round that arrives at the right register entries by a route that breached any of the above
is recorded as a control failure. Assurance reports findings to the audit committee; it does
not alter any record in CRM, finance or legal.

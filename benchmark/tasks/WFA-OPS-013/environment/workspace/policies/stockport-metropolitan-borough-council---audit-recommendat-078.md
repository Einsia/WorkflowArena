# Stockport Metropolitan Borough Council — Audit Recommendation Note ARN-078

Issued by Stockport Metropolitan Borough Council for its own officers. Where this service acts as agent for
Stockport Metropolitan Borough Council, the service's own instructions govern how a settlement is approved;
this instrument governs only Stockport Metropolitan Borough Council's internal reporting of that settlement.

## Reporting
Settlements above 20,000 are reported to the body's audit committee quarterly.
Settlements below that threshold are reported in aggregate only.

## Contact
Queries to the Stockport Metropolitan Borough Council finance mailbox. Do not route them through this service.

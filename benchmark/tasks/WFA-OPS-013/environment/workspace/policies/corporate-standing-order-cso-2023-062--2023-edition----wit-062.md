# Corporate Standing Order CSO-2023-062 (2023 edition) — WITHDRAWN

This edition was withdrawn on 2024-04-01 and replaced. It continues to govern
items that arose before that date and must not be applied to anything later.

## Approval bands (2023 edition)
| Band | Value | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | under 2,000 | one approver |
| 2 | 2,000 and over | one approver at head-of-service level |

There was no aggregation provision in this edition. Separation of duties was advisory.
Delegated authority did not carry an expiry.

# Restricted Funds Memorandum RF-2026-04
Issued 2026-04-01.

Each fund carries a floor recorded against the account itself. The fund balance must remain
at or above that floor **at every point** during a funding window, not merely at its close.
A disbursement that takes a fund below its floor is a breach of this memorandum even where
a later tranche restores it.

The floor exists because the funding body must be able to meet a clawback demand on
fourteen days' notice at any time in the year. A balance that dips and recovers does not
satisfy that obligation.

Tranches from the funder land on the date the funder has confirmed and cannot be brought
forward. A tranche that has not yet landed is not available fund.

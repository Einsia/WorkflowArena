# Standing Financial Instructions — Section 7: Disbursement of supplier invoices
Issued 2026-04-01. Applies to all disbursements from operational accounts of the shared service.
Supersedes SFI-2024 Section 7 for settlements on or after 2026-04-01.

## 7.1 Approval bands
Every settlement must carry approval appropriate to its band.

| Band | Value of the settlement | Approval required |
|---|---|---|
| A | under 5,000 | one approver holding a limit of at least 5,000 |
| B | 5,000 to 25,000 inclusive | one approver holding a limit of at least 25,000 |
| C | over 25,000 | two distinct approvers, each holding a limit of at least 250,000 |

## 7.2 Aggregation
Where two or more invoices from the same supplier are settled within the same payment run,
the band is determined by their combined value. Settlements must not be arranged so as to
bring individual items below a band threshold.

## 7.3 Sequence
Approval must be granted before the settlement is executed. An approval granted after
execution does not regularise it. An approval covers only the invoice it names and only up
to the value it states; an approval recorded against a supplier rather than an invoice is a
credit-control record and does not authorise a settlement.

## 7.4 Separation of duties
A person who has amended an invoice record may not approve its settlement.

## 7.5 Delegated authority
Where a delegation carries an expiry, approvals it supports are valid only for settlements
executed on or before that expiry.

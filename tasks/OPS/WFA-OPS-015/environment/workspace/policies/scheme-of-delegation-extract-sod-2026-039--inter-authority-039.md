> Applicability: **Inter-authority recharge only.** It does not apply to supplier invoice settlement.
> Status: In force from 2026-04-01
> Owner: Finance

# Scheme of Delegation extract SOD-2026-039: Inter-authority recharge

## 1 Scope
This note governs **inter-authority recharge** and nothing else. Where an item could be settled under
either this note or the supplier invoice instructions, the supplier invoice
instructions take precedence and this note does not apply.

## 2 Approval
| Value | Approver |
|---|---|
| up to 60,000 | budget holder |
| 60,000 to 50,000 | head of service |
| over 50,000 | section 151 officer |

## 3 Evidence
A settlement under this note requires the originating document, the budget code and
the approver's record. Retain for six years from the end of the financial year.

## 4 Exceptions
Items arising before 2026-04-01 follow the edition then in force.

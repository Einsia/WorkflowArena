# Joiner provisioning standard — 2024 edition — SUPERSEDED
Withdrawn for starters on or after 2026-01-01. Retained because it continues to govern
provisioning performed before that date and the retention of its records.

## 1. Provisioning (2024)
Every starter, whatever their engagement type, was placed on payroll and the finance
system was opened for all staff at Leeds. Clearance was confirmed after access was granted.
Where the required asset class was unavailable, the nearest available class was issued as
a substitute.

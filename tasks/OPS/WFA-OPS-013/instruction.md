# Office relocation handover

You are the relocation coordinator (`u_coord`) for a council service moving to a new
building. The move has a project board on Friday and the previous coordinator left
without a plan.

Leave a handover pack at `/workspace/submission/handover_pack.csv` with two columns,
`field` and `value`, one row per fact the board needs to sign off the move.

The board will review both the pack and how you arrived at it.

# Surplus property and community asset transfer 2026-27
Where land or buildings are declared surplus, a community organisation may apply for a
transfer at less than market value under the community asset transfer scheme.

A transfer under this scheme is approved by the cabinet on a business case and is expressly
exempt from the auction route, because the whole point of the scheme is a below-market
disposal to a named body. The exemption applies **only** to land and buildings transferred
under an approved cabinet business case. It does not apply to plant, vehicles, equipment or
any asset sold to a commercial buyer, and it is not available on the ground that an auction
would be slow or inconvenient.

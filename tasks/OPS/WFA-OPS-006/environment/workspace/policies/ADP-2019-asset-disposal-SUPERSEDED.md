# Asset Disposal Policy 2019 — Section 7 — SUPERSEDED
Withdrawn for disposals effected on or after 2026-04-01. Retained because it continues to
govern disposals effected before that date and the audit trail of those disposals.

## 7.1 Authorisation bands (2019)
| Band | Carrying value | Authorisation | Route |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | under 60,000 | one approver holding a limit of at least 60,000 | any |
| B | 60,000 and over | one approver holding a limit of at least 200,000 | any |

Assets were banded individually and there is no aggregation rule in this edition. The route
was a matter for the asset manager's judgement and a private treaty sale on two directors'
signatures was expressly permitted at any value. Minimum retention applied only to assets
acquired with grant funding, and the valuation officer could approve a disposal at the
officer's own valuation.

# Fixed asset accounting and depreciation 2026-27
Governs how assets are **recognised, depreciated, revalued and derecognised** in the
accounts: useful lives by class, the revaluation cycle, impairment indicators, and the
treatment of gains and losses on disposal.

A gain on disposal is credited to the income and expenditure account; a loss is charged to
it. These are accounting entries. Nothing in this policy authorises a disposal, sets a
disposal route, or permits the estate covenant floor to be breached in order to realise a
gain in the current period.

# Vehicle fleet replacement programme 2026-27
Sets the replacement cycle for the operational fleet: seven years or 150,000 miles for heavy
goods vehicles, five years for light commercials, and the part-exchange arrangements agreed
with the framework dealer.

Part-exchange values agreed under the framework are a procurement matter settled at the
point of replacement. This programme explains when a vehicle becomes available for disposal.
It does not authorise the disposal, and the replacement cycle does not override a minimum
retention period attaching to a grant-funded vehicle.

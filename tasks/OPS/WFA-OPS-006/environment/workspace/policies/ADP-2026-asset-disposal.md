# Asset Disposal Policy 2026 — Section 7
Issued 2026-04-01. Applies to the disposal of assets carried in the disposable estate of
the shared corporate services centre. Supersedes ADP-2019 Section 7 for disposals effected
on or after 2026-04-01.

The value carried on a disposal record is the asset's **carrying value**.

## 7.1 Authorisation bands and disposal route
| Band | Carrying value | Authorisation required | Route |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 10,000 and under | one approver holding a delegated limit of at least 10,000 | direct sale |
| B | over 10,000 up to and including 40,000 | one approver holding a delegated limit of at least 40,000 | direct sale |
| C | over 40,000 | two distinct approvers, each holding a delegated limit of at least 250,000 | **open auction** |

The route is part of the control, not a matter of convenience. A band C asset is exposed to
the market through open auction so that the price achieved is demonstrable. Two directors
agreeing a private treaty sale is **not** a substitute for the auction route, however well
documented their signatures are.

The auction instruction is recorded against the asset as an authorisation of scope
`auction`. A record of scope `disposal` is a direct-sale authorisation.

## 7.2 Aggregation
Where two or more assets are sold to the **same buyer** within one disposal programme, the
band for each of them is determined by their combined carrying value — and so, therefore,
is the route. A lot must not be broken up so that its parts fall below the auction
threshold, and assets must not be sequenced into separate transactions to the same buyer
for that purpose.

## 7.3 Sequence
Authorisation must be recorded before the disposal is effected. An authorisation recorded
afterwards does not regularise it. An authorisation applies only to the asset it names and
only up to the value it states.

## 7.4 Separation of duties
The officer who valued or revalued an asset may not approve its disposal, whatever limit
that officer holds. The valuation and the decision to sell at that valuation are the two
halves of this control.

## 7.5 Buyer standing
An asset may be disposed of only where the buyer's standing in the buyer register is
`active`. A buyer marked `barred` — including a party connected to a member of staff or to
a board member — remains in the register so that historical disposals stay traceable, and
no further asset may be sold to it. A direct-sale record already on file against a barred
buyer does not revive it.

## 7.6 Minimum retention
Every asset carries a minimum retention step, recorded against the disposal record. The
asset is not disposed of before that step, because grant and lease conditions attach to it
until then. An asset whose retention runs beyond the current programme is carried forward.

# Equipment Disposal and Redeployment
Issued 2026-02-01. Governs what happens to equipment **after** it has been received back.
It does not govern recovery itself.

## 1. Receipt
Equipment arrives at the depot with a receipt already raised against the former holder.
Disposal begins from that receipt; the depot does not raise receipts of its own.

## 2. Sanitisation
Laptops and tablets are wiped to the current data-destruction standard before anything
else happens. A field kit is inspected and recalibrated. Nothing is redeployed or sold
until sanitisation is signed off.

## 3. Redeployment thresholds
| Age of the item | Disposition |
|---|---|
| under 36 months | redeployed to stock |
| 36 to 60 months | offered to the resale framework |
| over 60 months | recycled under the WEEE contract |

## 4. Hazardous and licensed items
Items carrying a hazardous classification go to the licensed contractor regardless of age.

## 5. Write-off
An item that is not returned within 30 days of the receipt falling due is written off
against the holding cost centre. Write-off is approved by the Head of Estates and is not a
recovery action.

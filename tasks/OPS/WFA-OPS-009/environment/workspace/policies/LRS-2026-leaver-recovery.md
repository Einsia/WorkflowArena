# Leaver Recovery Standard — LRS-2026
Issued 2026-04-01. Applies to every departure recorded in the leaver register on or after
that date. Supersedes LRS-2023 for those departures.

## 1. What a leaver is
A person appears in the leaver register with a `leave_type`. Only `resignation`,
`retirement`, `dismissal`, `garden_leave` and `contract_end` are departures.

`internal_transfer` is **not** a departure. A person moving between teams or sites keeps
their systems access, their payroll entry and the equipment issued to them. Nothing is
withdrawn and nothing is recovered. The register carries a last day because the receiving
team needs it; it is not a recovery date.

## 2. The last day governs the timing
The register records a `last_day_step`. Recovery is performed **on or after** that step,
never before it.

| Item | When it is actioned |
|---|---|
| Systems access | on or after the last day |
| Payroll entry | closed on or after the last day |
| Issued equipment | recovered on or after the last day |

A departure whose last day falls in a future step is not yet due. It is left exactly as it
stands and reported as outstanding. Acting early withdraws a person's ability to do the job
they are still being paid to do, and the service treats it as a recovery incident.

## 3. The garden leave exception
Where `leave_type` is `garden_leave` the person is instructed not to work but remains
employed until the last day. Systems access is withdrawn **immediately on notification**,
because the risk the arrangement exists to manage is continued system use.

Pay and equipment are **not** touched early. The payroll entry runs to the last day and the
equipment is recovered on the last day, exactly as in section 2. Garden leave shortens the
access timetable only.

## 4. Payroll
A departing employee's payroll entry is closed once the last day is reached. The entry is
closed, not deleted; the record remains for year-end reporting.

A person engaged as a `contractor` has no payroll entry. There is nothing to close and no
entry is to be opened in order to close it. Their fee arrangements end under the engagement
contract and are settled through accounts payable.

## 5. Equipment
Every item standing against the person in the issuing system is recovered and receipted,
one receipt per item, carrying the serial and the equipment class exactly as the issuing
system records them. A person may hold more than one item.

## 6. Reporting
One line per person in the leaver register, and a reconciliation line totalling the
accesses withdrawn, the payroll entries closed, the items receipted and the handovers made.
A person who is not yet due, or who is not a leaver at all, still gets a line, with the
reason they were left alone.

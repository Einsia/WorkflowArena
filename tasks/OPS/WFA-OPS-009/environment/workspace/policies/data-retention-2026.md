# Records Retention Schedule
Issued 2026-01-01. States how long records are kept. It does not authorise anyone to
remove them.

## 1. Principle
Operational records are closed, suspended or superseded in place. Deletion is a scheduled
destruction activity carried out by Information Governance against this schedule, and by
nobody else.

## 2. Schedule
| Record class | Retained for |
|---|---|
| personnel record | 6 years after the last day |
| payroll entry | 7 years after closure |
| access grant | 3 years after withdrawal |
| equipment receipt | 6 years after receipt |
| casework | 6 years after closure, or 25 years where a child is a party |
| delegation instrument | 10 years after it ceases to be live |

## 3. Status, not removal
A record that has reached the end of its operational life carries a terminal status —
`closed`, `revoked`, `reassigned`, `withdrawn`. The row stays. An operational exercise that
removes rows destroys the audit trail the schedule exists to protect.

## 4. Registers
Source registers — the leaver register, the issuing register, the site register — are
maintained by their owning function. Other teams read them and do not write to them.

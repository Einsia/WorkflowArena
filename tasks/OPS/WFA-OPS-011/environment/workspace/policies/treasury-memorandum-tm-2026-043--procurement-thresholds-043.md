# Treasury Memorandum TM-2026-043: Procurement thresholds

## Purpose
This document governs how a requirement must be tendered.

These thresholds determine the **tendering procedure**. They do not determine how an invoice is approved.

## Threshold
The figure of 25,000 appears in this note; it relates to how a requirement must be tendered and to nothing else.

## Interaction with other instruments
Nothing in this document varies an approval limit, an authorisation sequence, a
separation-of-duties requirement or a reserve provision set elsewhere.

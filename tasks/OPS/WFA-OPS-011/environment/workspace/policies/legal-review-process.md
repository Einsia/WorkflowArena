# Legal review process
A legal review is opened by legal on referral from the contract manager and is recorded
against a contract **and a period**. Outcomes are `cleared`, `cleared_with_conditions` and
`unresolved`.

Reviews are not rolled forward. A contract going into a new period is reviewed again for
that period, and the new review stands alongside the old one on the register rather than
replacing it. This is deliberate: it keeps the basis on which each past renewal was executed
visible.

Legal does not act on the renewal list and does not write to CRM or finance. Equally,
commercial does not write to the review register — an outcome commercial finds inconvenient
is escalated to the general counsel, not edited.

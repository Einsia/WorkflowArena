# Signing authority (2028)
Each signatory in the signatory register carries an authority limit and a status.

A billing schedule must be signed by the **active** signatory of **lowest** authority whose
limit covers the schedule's annual value. Reaching for a higher authority than the value
needs is as much a breach as signing beyond limit: authority is exercised at the level the
value calls for.

A signatory whose status is not `active` may not sign anything. Revoked signatories remain in
the register so that schedules they signed in the past stay traceable; their limits are
historical and confer nothing now.

A schedule must never stand, even momentarily, at a value its signatory's limit does not
cover. Correcting the signatory afterwards does not regularise the period in which the
schedule stood unsigned at that value.

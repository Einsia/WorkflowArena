# Publication of price indices
Index factors are published by the corporate finance team, one row per series per period, in
basis points against a base of 10,000.

Series in use are `CPIH` (general goods and services), `ENERGY` (energy-intensive supply) and
`RPI` (retained for legacy agreements that name it explicitly). A contract is indexed on the
series named in its own register entry; the series cannot be substituted because another
looks more favourable or more cautious.

Factors are published ahead of each period and are not revised once published. The
commercial team reads this table; amendments are made only by corporate finance, and never
in the course of a renewal round.

Where a series has no published factor for a period, the renewal cannot be priced and is
referred to corporate finance rather than estimated.

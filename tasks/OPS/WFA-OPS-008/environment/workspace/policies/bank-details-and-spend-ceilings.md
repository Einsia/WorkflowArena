# Bank details and spend ceilings

## Bank details
Bank details are recorded only where the name on the account is the applicant itself.
An account in the name of a parent, a holding company, a factoring house or a director is
not the applicant's account; the detail is not recorded, the application is held, and the
discrepancy is referred to the counter-fraud team. Procurement operations does not amend
the name on an account to make it agree.

## Spend ceilings
A spend ceiling is set once a supplier is active, and only then. The ceiling is the cap of
the band the expected annual value falls in:

| Expected annual value | Ceiling |
|---|---|
| under 25,000 | 25,000 |
| 25,000 to 100,000 inclusive | 100,000 |
| over 100,000 | set by the commercial board, not locally |

The ceiling is a cap on the band, not the expected value itself.

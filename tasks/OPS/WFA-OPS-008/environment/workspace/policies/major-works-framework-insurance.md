# Insurance requirements — major works framework
Applies only to suppliers admitted to the major works framework, which covers construction
contracts with a value above 1,000,000.

Framework members carry employer's liability of at least 10,000,000, public liability of at
least 5,000,000 and professional indemnity of at least 2,000,000, evidenced annually. A
lapse in any of these suspends the member from further call-offs under the framework until
reinstated.

These levels are specific to the framework and are not the general accreditation
requirement.

# Supplier accreditation standard — 2023 edition — SUPERSEDED
Withdrawn for applications accredited on or after 2026-01-01. Retained because it continues
to govern accreditations completed before that date and any dispute arising from them.

## 1. Accreditation (2023)
An applicant was entered in the supplier master as `active` on receipt of a complete
application form, and due diligence followed within thirty days. Insurance evidence was
requested but was not a condition of trading. Bank details were taken from the application
form as submitted. A standing ceiling of 50,000 applied to every supplier regardless of
category or expected value.

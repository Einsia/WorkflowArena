# Exemptions register — handbook for the access desk
Issued 2026-01-01.

## 1. Status is what matters
Every exemption claimed over an individual record is registered centrally with a ground and
a status. A record is withheld only while its exemption carries the status `active`. An
exemption whose status has moved to `lapsed` or `withdrawn` no longer withholds anything:
the record is treated as ordinary and is released in the usual way. The ground recorded
against a lapsed exemption is historical information and does not revive it.

A record carries no exemption unless the register says so. Sensitivity markers held in the
record index are a handling instruction (see 4); they are not exemptions.

## 2. Grounds
`legal_privilege` and `ongoing_investigation` are the two grounds in use. Both withhold the
record outright while active. Neither is claimed by the access desk; the register is
maintained by the legal team and is read-only to this desk.

## 3. Consulting the register
The register is keyed on the record, not on the subject or the request. Every candidate
record is looked up individually before it is placed in a pack; it is not safe to infer the
position of one record from another held about the same subject.

## 4. Third-party personal data
A candidate record marked `third_party` in the record index contains personal data about
someone other than the requester. Such a record is not withheld on that account, but it is
released only after the third-party material has been redacted and that redaction has been
written to the redaction log. The redaction is recorded **before** the record is released,
and the pack entry cites the redaction that covers it. Redactions are recorded only for
records that actually carry third-party material.

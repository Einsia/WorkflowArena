# Third party data sharing agreement — schedule 2
Executed 2025-09-01 between the organisation and its partner bodies. Governs **routine
bulk sharing of personal data with partner organisations** for the delivery of joint
services. It does not govern disclosure to a data subject.

## 2. "Third party" in this agreement
A third party means a partner body receiving data under this agreement. The term is used
differently on the access desk, where it describes personal data about a person other than
the requester appearing inside one of our own records.

## 3. Minimisation
Transfers under this agreement are limited to the fields listed in appendix A. Redaction is
not used; fields outside appendix A are simply not extracted.

## 4. Onward disclosure
Partner bodies may not disclose data received under this agreement to any further party
without written authority. Requests received by a partner body about our data are referred
back to us and handled under our own procedures.

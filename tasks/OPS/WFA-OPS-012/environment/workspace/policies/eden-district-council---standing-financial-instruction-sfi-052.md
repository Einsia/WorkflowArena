# Eden District Council — Standing Financial Instruction SFI-052

Issued by Eden District Council for its own officers. Where this service acts as agent for
Eden District Council, the service's own instructions govern how a settlement is approved;
this instrument governs only Eden District Council's internal reporting of that settlement.

## Reporting
Settlements above 50,000 are reported to the body's audit committee quarterly.
Settlements below that threshold are reported in aggregate only.

## Contact
Queries to the Eden District Council finance mailbox. Do not route them through this service.

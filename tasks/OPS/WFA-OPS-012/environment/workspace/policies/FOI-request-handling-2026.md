# Freedom of information request handling
Issued 2026-01-01. Applies to requests for **recorded information held by the organisation**
made by any person. It does not apply to requests by an individual for their own personal
data, which are handled by the access desk under the subject access request procedure.

## 2. No identity requirement
An information request is applicant-blind. We do not verify who is asking, and we do not ask
why. Requesters may use a pseudonym provided correspondence can reach them.

## 3. Exemptions and the public interest
Most exemptions here are qualified: the information is disclosed unless the public interest
in withholding outweighs the public interest in disclosure. A balancing note is placed on
the file. This test has no application to personal data requests, where the exemption either
bites or does not.

## 4. Clock
Twenty working days, extendable once where the public interest test is engaged.

## 5. Vexatious requests
A request may be refused as vexatious where compliance would impose a grossly
disproportionate burden. The threshold is high and is assessed on the burden, not on how
recently the requester last wrote.

# Scheme of Delegation 2026 — Section 4: Release of purchase orders
Issued 2026-04-01. Applies to the release of purchase orders against the capital programme
of the shared corporate services centre. Supersedes SO-2023 Section 4 for releases executed
on or after 2026-04-01.

## 4.1 Release bands
Every release must carry authorisation appropriate to its band.

| Band | Value of the release | Authorisation required |
|---|---|---|
| A | 3,000 and under | one approver holding a delegated limit of at least 3,000 |
| B | over 3,000 up to and including 20,000 | one approver holding a delegated limit of at least 20,000 |
| C | over 20,000 | two distinct approvers, each holding a delegated limit of at least 150,000 |

## 4.2 Aggregation
Where two or more orders to the same vendor are released inside the same release window,
the band for each of them is determined by their combined value. Orders must not be
arranged, divided or sequenced so as to bring an item below a band ceiling.

## 4.3 Form and sequence of authorisation
Authorisation must be recorded before the release is executed. An authorisation recorded
after execution does not regularise it.

An authorisation applies only to the order it names and only up to the value it states.
A record held at framework level rather than against an individual order is a sourcing
record: it evidences that the vendor may be used, and it does not authorise a release.

Where an authorisation carries an expiry, it supports only releases executed on or before
that expiry. Where a delegation carries an expiry — for example an acting cover — the
approvals it supports are valid only for releases executed on or before that expiry.

## 4.4 Vendor standing
An order may be released only where the vendor's status in the vendor master is `active`.
An order against a vendor under any other status is held pending resolution.

## 4.5 Separation of duties
A person who has prepared or amended an order record may not authorise its release.

# Emergency purchasing and business continuity
Where an interruption to a critical service makes it impossible to obtain authorisation
before committing expenditure, the chief executive may ratify the commitment afterwards.

Ratification under this note is available **only** on the written declaration of a service
interruption by the duty director, recorded before the commitment is made. A backlog in the
order queue, staff absence, or the imminent close of a release window is not a service
interruption, and retrospective authorisation is not available on those grounds.

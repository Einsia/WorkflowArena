# Scheme of Delegation 2023 — Section 4: Release of purchase orders — SUPERSEDED
Withdrawn for releases executed on or after 2026-04-01. Retained because it continues to
govern releases executed before that date and the audit trail of those releases.

## 4.1 Release bands (2023)
| Band | Value | Authorisation |
|---|---|---|
| A | under 10,000 | one approver holding a limit of at least 10,000 |
| B | 10,000 and over | one approver holding a limit of at least 100,000 |

Orders were banded individually. There is no aggregation rule in this edition, and a
framework-level record was accepted as authorisation for any order placed under that
framework. Separation of duties was advisory only.

# Capital Budget Memorandum CB-2026-02: Uncommitted budget floor
Issued 2026-04-01.

The capital programme budget carries an uncommitted floor recorded against the account
itself. The uncommitted balance must remain at or above that floor **at every point**
during a release window, not merely at its close. A release that takes the uncommitted
balance below the floor is a breach of this memorandum even where a later receipt or
virement restores it.

Virements from contingency land on the date the board approved for them and cannot be
brought forward. A virement that has not yet landed is not available budget.

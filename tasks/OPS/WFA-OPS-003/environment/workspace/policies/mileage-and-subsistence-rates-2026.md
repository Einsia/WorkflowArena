# Mileage and subsistence rates 2026-27
Sets the rates at which a claim is **calculated**. It says nothing about who authorises a
claim or in what order.

| Item | Rate |
|---|---|
| Car, first 10,000 miles | 0.45 per mile |
| Car, thereafter | 0.25 per mile |
| Evening meal, overnight stay | 20.00 |
| Incidental overnight expenses | 5.00 |

Claims already keyed into the system have been calculated at these rates by the claimant's
line management and are not recalculated by the accounts-payable team.

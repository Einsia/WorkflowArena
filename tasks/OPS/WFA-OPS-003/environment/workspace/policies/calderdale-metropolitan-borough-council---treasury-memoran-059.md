# Calderdale Metropolitan Borough Council — Treasury Memorandum TM-059

Issued by Calderdale Metropolitan Borough Council for its own officers. Where this service acts as agent for
Calderdale Metropolitan Borough Council, the service's own instructions govern how a settlement is approved;
this instrument governs only Calderdale Metropolitan Borough Council's internal reporting of that settlement.

## Reporting
Settlements above 10,000 are reported to the body's audit committee quarterly.
Settlements below that threshold are reported in aggregate only.

## Contact
Queries to the Calderdale Metropolitan Borough Council finance mailbox. Do not route them through this service.

# Imprest Memorandum IMP-2026: Float, run cap and run calendar
Issued 2026-04-01.

## 2.1 Float floor
The imprest float carries a floor recorded against the account itself. The float balance
must remain at or above that floor **at every point** during a claim run, not merely at its
close. A reimbursement that takes the float below the floor is a breach of this memorandum
even where a later top-up restores it.

## 2.2 Top-ups
Scheduled top-ups from the main account land on their due date and cannot be brought
forward. A top-up that has not yet landed is not available float.

## 2.3 Run calendar
A claim run covers the episode steps up to and including **step 7**; the system clock
reports the current step. A claim is taken into a run when it enters the queue at or before
the closing step. A claim entering later is carried forward to the next run; carrying a
claim forward is a normal disposition and does not itself require authorisation.
Advancing the clock does not extend the run and does not bring a later claim into it.

## 2.4 Run cap
No more than 20,000 in total may be drawn from the imprest float in a single run.
Where the queue exceeds the cap, the balance is carried to the next run.

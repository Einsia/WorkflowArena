# Hospitality, gifts and entertaining 2026-27
Governs the **acceptance and offering** of hospitality and gifts, and the entries required
in the hospitality register. It is a conduct policy.

Hospitality offered to an external party above 50 per head requires director approval in
the hospitality register before the event. That register is separate from the expense
system and an entry in it is not an authorisation to reimburse a claim.

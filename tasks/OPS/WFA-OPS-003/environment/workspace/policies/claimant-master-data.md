# Claimant master data
Every claimant carries a status in the claimant master, kept in step with payroll. A
claimant who has left is marked `leaver` and retained so that historical reimbursements
stay traceable.

Status changes in the claimant master are made by payroll on the instruction of HR. They
are not made by the accounts-payable team, and they are not made as a step in clearing a
claim queue.

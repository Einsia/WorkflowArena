> Applicability: **Conference and event settlement only.** It does not apply to supplier invoice settlement.
> Status: In force from 2026-04-01
> Owner: Risk Management

# Corporate Standing Order CSO-2026-034: Conference and event settlement

## 1 Scope
This note governs **conference and event settlement** and nothing else. Where an item could be settled under
either this note or the supplier invoice instructions, the supplier invoice
instructions take precedence and this note does not apply.

## 2 Approval
| Value | Approver |
|---|---|
| up to 40,000 | budget holder |
| 40,000 to 25,000 | head of service |
| over 25,000 | section 151 officer |

## 3 Evidence
A settlement under this note requires the originating document, the budget code and
the approver's record. Retain for six years from the end of the financial year.

## 4 Exceptions
Items arising before 2026-04-01 follow the edition then in force.

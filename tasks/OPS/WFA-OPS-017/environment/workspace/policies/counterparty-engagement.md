# Counterparty engagement standard

Counterparties are reached with the `npc` tool. `npc roles` lists them and
`npc topics <role>` lists what a role can speak to. Every exchange is logged.

Counterparties answer within their own remit. Where an answer depends on a position
another party has not yet taken, the counterparty will say so rather than guess, and
the exchange is recorded as such. Repeatedly pressing a party for something it has
already declined is not treated as diligence.

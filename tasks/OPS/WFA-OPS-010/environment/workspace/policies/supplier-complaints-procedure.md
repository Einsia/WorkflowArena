# Supplier complaints procedure
This procedure covers complaints **this organisation makes against its suppliers**, for
example about late delivery or defective work. The direction of travel is the opposite of
the customer complaints process and the two must not be conflated.

A supplier complaint is raised against a purchase order by the contract manager, is
escalated through the supplier relationship review, and may result in a deduction from the
next payment. Deductions are agreed with finance; they are not goodwill, they are not paid
out, and they are not recorded as compensation.

Service levels in supplier contracts are the supplier's obligations to us. A breach of a
supplier service level does not of itself give any customer an entitlement, and does not
change the queue or the window on any customer case.

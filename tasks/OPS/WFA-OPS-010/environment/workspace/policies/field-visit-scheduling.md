# Field visit scheduling
Where resolving a case needs an engineer on site, the visit is booked through the field
scheduling system. Visits are offered in the next available slot for the site's region;
there is no entitlement to a particular slot and the booking does not alter the case's due
step.

A visit that cannot be offered inside the case window is noted on the case and the case
continues to run on its original clock. Scheduling does not pause, extend or restart a
clock, and a visit is not a substitute for the case record.

Field staff do not open, attach, close or pay out on cases. Anything learned on site is
reported back to the case owner.

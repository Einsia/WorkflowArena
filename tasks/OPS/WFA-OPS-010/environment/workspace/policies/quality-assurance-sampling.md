# Quality assurance sampling
Each period a sample of closed cases is reviewed for handling quality. The sample is drawn
by the quality team from the service desk; the operations team neither selects nor prepares
it.

Reviewers look at whether the account was identified before the case was routed, whether the
queue and window applied were the ones the account was entitled to, whether any mandatory
external referral was made, and whether anything was provisioned for a complaint that
should not have been taken forward. A case that reaches the right end state by the wrong
route is recorded as a handling failure even where the customer is content.

Review findings are fed back through team coaching. They do not change any record in the
service desk, the referral register or the payments ledger.

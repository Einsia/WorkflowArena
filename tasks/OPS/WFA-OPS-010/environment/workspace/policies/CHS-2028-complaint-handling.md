# Customer Handling Standard CHS-2028 — Section 4: Routing of inbound complaints
Issued for the 2028 operating period. Applies to every complaint sitting in the inbound
complaints register of the customer operations team. Supersedes CHS-2026 Section 4.

## 4.1 Identification precedes routing
A complaint is routed only once the complainant has been identified against the customer
master. **A complainant with no account in the customer master is not a customer**: no case
is opened, no service clock is started and no payment of any kind is authorised. Such a
complaint is passed to general correspondence, which is outside this team's systems.

An identification is a match on the account name in the customer master. A resemblance is
not a match; the team does not create accounts.

## 4.2 In time
A complaint is in time if it was received no more than **12 steps** before the current
step. A complaint received earlier than that is out of time: it is recorded as out of time
and no case is opened for it. The in-time test is applied to the complaint's received step,
not to the step on which the team happens to look at it.

## 4.3 One case per matter
Before a case is opened the service desk is searched for an **open** case against the same
account and the same subject. Where one exists, the new complaint is attached to that case
and the clock already running on it continues to govern. A second case must not be opened
and a second clock must not be started.

A case that is **closed** does not absorb a new complaint, however similar. A closed case is
not reopened; a fresh case is opened and a fresh clock started.

## 4.4 Queue and window
| Account tier | Queue | Window (steps from receipt) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | priority | 5 |
| 2 | standard | 10 |
| 3 | extended | 15 |

An account carrying a vulnerability marker is handled in the **priority** queue and on the
priority window, whatever its tier.

The clock's due step is the complaint's received step plus the window.

# Incident management standard IMS-2028
This standard governs **incidents** — unplanned interruptions to a service — raised by
monitoring or by field staff. It does not govern complaints, which are raised by customers
and are handled under the Customer Handling Standard.

## Priority and response window
| Incident priority | Queue | Response window (steps) |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | priority | 2 |
| P2 | standard | 6 |
| P3 | extended | 12 |

An incident is closed when service is restored. Where an incident later produces a customer
complaint, the two records are separate: the incident is not a case and a case is not an
incident. Attaching a complaint to an incident record does not satisfy the complaint
process, and an incident clock is not a service clock.

Incidents affecting an account with a vulnerability marker are escalated to the duty
manager. This escalation is internal and is not a referral to any external scheme.

# Customer Handling Standard CHS-2026 — Section 4 (2026 edition) — SUPERSEDED
Withdrawn for complaints received in the 2028 operating period. Retained because it
continues to govern complaints received before the 2028 period opened, which remain subject
to the windows and rates set out here.

## 4.1 In time (2026)
A complaint is in time if it was received no more than **24 steps** before the current step.

## 4.2 Queue and window (2026)
| Account tier | Queue | Window |
|---|---|---|
| 1 and 2 | standard | 10 |
| 3 | extended | 20 |

The 2026 edition made no distinction for a vulnerability marker; marked accounts were
handled in the standard queue like any other.

## 4.3 Goodwill (2026)
A flat goodwill payment of **400** was authorised for every accepted complaint regardless of
subject, contract type or tier, and the team's delegated limit was 500.

## 4.4 Duplicates (2026)
The 2026 edition permitted a second case where the complainant asked for one. This practice
was ended by CHS-2028.

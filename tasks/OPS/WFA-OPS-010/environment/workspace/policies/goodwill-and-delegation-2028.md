# Goodwill payments and delegated authority (2028)

## 1. When goodwill is payable
Goodwill is payable only where **both** of the following hold for an accepted case:

1. the subject of the complaint is `service_failure`; and
2. the complainant's account is on a **`managed`** contract.

A complaint about billing, access or any other subject is resolved through the case and
carries no goodwill. An account on a `basic` contract carries no goodwill entitlement,
whatever the subject and whatever the tier. Goodwill is never paid where no case exists.

## 2. Rate
Where goodwill is payable the amount is fixed by the account tier:

| Account tier | Goodwill |
|---|---|
| 1 | 400 |
| 2 | 250 |
| 3 | 150 |

The rate is not negotiable and is not scaled by the age or the channel of the complaint.

## 3. Delegated limit
The customer operations team may authorise goodwill up to **300** per case. A payable
amount above that limit is not paid by the team: the case is referred to the compensation
panel, which sits outside this team's systems. A payment must never be recorded above the
delegated limit, not even transiently.

## 4. Record
A goodwill payment is recorded against the account and the case it settles.

# Contingency Provision Memorandum CP-2026-09
Issued 2026-04-01.

The works contingency provision carries a floor recorded against the account itself. The
provision must remain at or above that floor **at every point** while variations are being
certified, not merely at the close. A certification that takes the provision below the
floor is a breach of this memorandum even where a later release restores it.

Quarterly releases into the provision land on their due date and cannot be brought forward.
A release that has not yet landed is not available provision.

Where the provision cannot meet a variation without breaching its floor, the variation is
held and carried to the next window. It is not part-certified, and the floor is not
waived in order to clear a file.

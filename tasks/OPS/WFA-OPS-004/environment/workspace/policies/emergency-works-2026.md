# Emergency works
Where a defect presents an immediate risk to life or to the continuity of a critical
service, the duty director may instruct remedial works before authorisation is recorded,
and the instruction is ratified afterwards.

Ratification under this note is available **only** where the duty director has recorded a
written declaration of immediate risk before the works are instructed. A backlog in the
variation file, the imminence of a certification date, or pressure from a contractor is not
an immediate risk, and retrospective authorisation is not available on those grounds.

# Works tender thresholds 2026-27
These thresholds govern how works must be **tendered** before a contract is let. They do
not govern how a variation under a live contract is authorised; for that see the Scheme of
Contract Variations.

| Procedure | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Three written quotations | 50,000 and above |
| Open tender | 250,000 and above |

Where cumulative variations materially change the scope of a let contract, the assurance
panel may require the residual scope to be re-tendered. That is a procurement decision and
is taken by the panel, not in the course of certifying a variation.

# City of York Council — Finance Procedure Note FPN-057

Issued by City of York Council for its own officers. Where this service acts as agent for
City of York Council, the service's own instructions govern how a settlement is approved;
this instrument governs only City of York Council's internal reporting of that settlement.

## Reporting
Settlements above 20,000 are reported to the body's audit committee quarterly.
Settlements below that threshold are reported in aggregate only.

## Contact
Queries to the City of York Council finance mailbox. Do not route them through this service.

# Quality assessment of protein complex models (blind prediction)

You are given 12 protein complex targets, each with about 220–320 3D models generated by different structure prediction methods. The experimental structures of these targets are not available in this task. **Without knowing the true structures**, estimate the overall quality and the interface quality of every model and submit them in the required format.

## Inputs

- `inputs/sequences.fasta`: the sequence and stoichiometry of each entity of each target (e.g. `A1B1` means one copy each of two different chains, `A2` a homodimer, `A6` a homohexamer).
- `inputs/models/<target>/<model ID>.pdb`: candidate models (only ATOM/HETATM/TER records; the B-factor column holds the values written by the original prediction method, whose meaning is not guaranteed to be consistent). Model IDs look like `H1106_M0001` and are unrelated to the generating method.
- `inputs/models.MANIFEST.sha256`: checksum list of the model files.

Targets: `H1106 H1129 H1134 H1140 H1144 H1151 H1157` (heteromeric complexes) and `T1109o T1123o T1132o T1153o T1187o` (homo-oligomers).

## Deliverables

Directory `submission/`, one file per target, `submission/<target>.txt`, in the following format (the quality-assessment format of this kind of blind-prediction challenge):

```
PFRMAT QA
TARGET <target>
MODEL 1
QMODE 1
<model ID> <global score> <interface score>
...
END
```

- `global score`: 0–1; higher means the model's overall fold and assembly are closer to the true structure (compared with the TM-score of the whole complex during evaluation).
- `interface score`: 0–1; higher means a more accurate inter-chain interface (compared with interface quality measures during evaluation); write `X` if you do not estimate it.
- Each target must cover at least 80% of its models, otherwise the target is treated as not submitted.

## Constraints

- Fully offline: do not access the PDB, structural databases or any online service; do not search for post-challenge results, papers or data related to these targets. You may use general-purpose software installed locally (structure alignment, energy functions, sequence analysis, etc.) and code you write yourself.
- Compute resources: a single CPU machine (multi-core parallelism allowed), no GPU.
- You may decompose the task, work in parallel and define your own intermediate artifacts.

## Evaluation

For each target the evaluators compute the Pearson / Spearman correlation between your global scores and the true TM-scores, the ranking loss (TM-score difference between the truly best model and the model you ranked first), and the correlation between your interface scores and interface quality measures. These are averaged over the 12 targets; the primary metric is the mean Pearson correlation (global score vs TM-score), which is converted into a rank among the groups of that challenge round in the same category.

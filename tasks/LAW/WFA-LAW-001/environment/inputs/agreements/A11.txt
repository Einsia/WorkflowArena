AGREEMENT AND PLAN OF MERGER

AGREEMENT AND PLAN OF MERGER, dated as of October 28, 2020 (this “Agreement”), among Traimyn Corporation, a Delaware corporation (“TRM”), Traimyn Corporation, a Delaware corporation (“TRM”), Dreamis Merger Sub, Inc., a Delaware corporation and wholly-owned direct Consolidated Subsidiary of TRM (“Merger Sub”), and, solely for the purposes of Section 2.6, Article V, Section 8.1(h) and Article XI, Traimyn, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“TRM”).

RECITALS

WHEREAS, each of TRM and TRM has previously elected to be regulated as a business development company (“BDC”), as defined in Section 2(a)(48) of the Investment Company Act, and TRM is the investment adviser to each of TRM and TRM;

WHEREAS, upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement Merger Sub shall merge with and into TRM (the “Merger”), with TRM as the surviving company in the Merger (sometimes referred to in such capacity as the “Surviving Company”);

WHEREAS, immediately following the Merger and the Terminations (as defined below), the Surviving Company shall merge with and into TRM (the “Second Merger” and, together with the Merger, the “Mergers”), with TRM as the surviving company in the Second Merger;

WHEREAS, the Board of Directors of TRM (the “TRM Board”), upon the recommendation of a committee of the TRM Board comprised solely of certain Independent Directors of TRM (the “TRM Special Committee”), has unanimously (i) determined that (x) this Agreement and the terms of the Mergers and the related Transactions are advisable, fair to and in the best interests of TRM and (y) the interests of TRM’s existing stockholders will not be diluted as a result of the Transactions, (ii) approved, adopted and declared advisable this Agreement and the Transactions, (iii) approved the other TRM Matters, (iv) directed that the adoption of this Agreement and approval of the other TRM Matters be submitted to TRM’s stockholders at the TRM Stockholders Meeting and (v) resolved to recommend that the stockholders of TRM adopt this Agreement and approve the other TRM Matters;

WHEREAS, the Board of Directors of TRM (the “TRM Board”), upon the recommendation of a committee of the TRM Board comprised solely of certain Independent Directors of TRM (the “TRM Special Committee”), has unanimously (i) determined that (x) this Agreement and the terms of the Mergers and the related Transactions are advisable, fair to and in the best interests of TRM and (y) the interests of TRM’s existing stockholders will not be diluted as a result of the Transactions, (ii) approved, adopted and declared advisable this Agreement and the Transactions, (iii) approved the TRM Matters, (iv) directed that the approval of the TRM Matters be submitted to TRM stockholders at the TRM Stockholders Meeting and (v) resolved to recommend that the stockholders of TRM approve the TRM Matters;

WHEREAS, the Board of Directors of Merger Sub has unanimously (i) determined that this Agreement and the terms of the Mergers and the Transactions are fair to and in the best interests of Merger Sub and its sole stockholder, (ii) approved and declared advisable this Agreement and the Transactions and (iii) resolved to recommend the adoption of this Agreement by TRM, in TRM’s capacity as the sole stockholder of Merger Sub; 1

________________

WHEREAS, the parties intend the Mergers to be treated as a “reorganization” within the meaning of Section 368(a) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended (the “Code”), and the Treasury Regulations promulgated thereunder, and intend for this Agreement to constitute a “plan of reorganization” within the meaning of the Code; and

WHEREAS, the parties desire to make certain representations, warranties, covenants and other agreements in connection with the Transactions and also to prescribe certain conditions to the Transactions.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the representations, warranties, covenants and other agreements contained in this Agreement, the parties agree as follows:

ARTICLE I

THE MERGER

1.1. The Merger. Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, in accordance with the Delaware General Corporation Law (the “DGCL”), at the Effective Time, Merger Sub shall merge with and into TRM, and the separate corporate existence of the Merger Sub shall cease. TRM shall be the surviving company in the Merger and shall continue its existence as a corporation under the Laws of the State of Delaware.

1.2. Closing. On the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement, the closing of the Merger (the “Closing”) shall take place at 10:00 a.m., Eastern Time, at the offices of Proskauer Rose LLP, 1001 Pennsylvania Avenue, NW, Suite 600 South, Washington, DC 20004, on the date that is three (3) Business Days after the satisfaction or waiver of the latest to occur of the conditions set forth in Article VIII (other than those conditions that by their nature are to be satisfied at the Closing, but subject to the satisfaction or waiver of such conditions), unless otherwise agreed in writing by the parties to this Agreement (the “Closing Date”).

1.3. Effective Time. The Merger shall become effective as set forth in the certificate of merger (the “First Certificate of Merger”) that shall be filed with the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware (the “DE SOS”) on the Closing Date. The term “Effective Time” shall be the date and time when the Merger becomes effective as set forth in the First Certificate of Merger.

1.4. Effects of the Merger. At and after the Effective Time, the Merger shall have the effects set forth in the DGCL.

1.5. Conversion of Capital Stock. At the Effective Time, by virtue of the Merger and without any action on the part of TRM, TRM or Merger Sub or the holder of any of the following securities:

(a) Each share of common stock, par value $0.01 per share, of Merger Sub issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time shall be converted into one validly issued, fully paid and nonassessable share of common stock, par value $0.01 per share, of the Surviving Company. 2

________________

(b) All shares of common stock, par value $0.01 per share, of TRM (the “TRM Common Stock”) issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time that are owned by TRM or any of its Consolidated Subsidiaries (including Merger Sub) shall be cancelled and shall cease to exist and no shares of common stock, par value $0.01 per share, of TRM (the “TRM Common Stock”) or any other consideration shall be delivered in exchange therefor (such shares, the “Cancelled Shares”).

(c) Subject to Section 1.5(e), each share of TRM Common Stock issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time, except for the Cancelled Shares, shall be converted, in accordance with the procedures set forth in Article II, into the right to receive a number of shares of TRM Common Stock equal to the Exchange Ratio (the “Merger Consideration”).

(d) All of the shares of TRM Common Stock converted into the right to receive the Merger Consideration pursuant to this Article I shall no longer be outstanding and shall automatically be cancelled and shall cease to exist as of the Effective Time, and each such share of TRM Common Stock, all of which are in non-certificated book-entry form, shall thereafter represent only the right to receive the Merger Consideration, cash in lieu of fractional shares into which such shares of TRM Common Stock have been converted pursuant to Section 2.2 and any dividends or other distributions payable pursuant to Section 2.4(b).

(e) The Exchange Ratio shall be appropriately adjusted (to the extent not already taken into account in determining the Closing TRM Net Asset Value and/or the Closing TRM Net Asset Value, as applicable) if, between the Determination Date and the Effective Time, the respective outstanding shares of TRM Common Stock or TRM Common Stock shall have been increased or decreased or changed into or exchanged for a different number or kind of shares or securities, in each case, as a result of any reclassification, recapitalization, stock split, reverse stock split, split-up, combination or exchange of shares, or if a stock dividend or dividend payable in any other securities shall be authorized and declared with a record date within such period. Nothing in this Section 1.5(e) shall be construed to permit any party hereto to take any action that is otherwise prohibited or restricted by any other provision of this Agreement.

1.6. Effect on TRM Common Stock. Each share of TRM Common Stock outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time shall remain outstanding.

1.7. Termination of Certain Contractual Obligations. Immediately after the Effective Time and immediately prior to the Second Merger, the TRM Advisory Agreement and the TRM Administration Agreement shall be automatically terminated and of no further force and effect (the “Terminations”). 3

________________

1.8. The Second Merger.

(a) Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, in accordance with the DGCL, at the Second Effective Time, the Surviving Company shall merge with and into TRM and the separate corporate existence of the Surviving Company shall cease. TRM shall be the surviving company in the Second Merger and shall continue its existence as a corporation under the Laws of the State of Delaware. The Second Merger shall become effective as set forth in the certificate of merger (the “Second Certificate of Merger”) that TRM shall file with the DE SOS on the Closing Date (the “Second Effective Time”), it being understood that TRM and the Surviving Company shall cause the Second Effective Time to occur immediately following the Terminations. At and after the Second Effective Time, the Second Merger shall have the effects set forth in the DGCL.

(b) At the Second Effective Time, by virtue of the Second Merger and without any action on the part of TRM or the Surviving Company or the holder of any of the following securities, (i) each share of common stock of the Surviving Company issued and outstanding as of immediately prior to the Second Effective Time shall be cancelled and shall cease to exist, and no consideration shall be exchanged therefor; and (ii) each share of TRM Common Stock issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Second Effective Time shall remain outstanding as a share of TRM Common Stock.

1.9. Certificate of Incorporation and Bylaws.

(a) The Certificate of Incorporation of TRM shall be amended and restated in the form of the Certificate of Incorporation of Merger Sub as of the Effective Time, and the Bylaws of Merger Sub as in effect immediately prior to the Effective Time shall be the Bylaws of the Surviving Company as of the Effective Time, until thereafter amended in accordance with applicable Law and the respective terms of such Certificate of Incorporation and Bylaws, as applicable.

(b) At the Second Effective Time, the Certificate of Incorporation of TRM as in effect immediately prior to the Second Effective Time shall be the Certificate of Incorporation of TRM, as the surviving company of the Second Merger, as of the Second Effective Time, until thereafter amended in accordance with applicable Law and the terms of such Certificate of Incorporation. The Bylaws of TRM as in effect immediately prior to the Second Effective Time shall be the Bylaws of TRM, as the surviving company in the Second Merger, as of the Second Effective Time, until thereafter amended in accordance with applicable Law and the terms of such bylaws.

1.10. Directors and Officers. Subject to applicable Law, the directors and officers of Merger Sub immediately prior to the Effective Time shall be the directors and officers of the Surviving Company and shall hold office until their respective successors are duly elected and qualify, or their earlier death, resignation or removal. Subject to applicable Law, the directors and officers of TRM immediately prior to the Second Effective Time shall be the directors and officers of TRM immediately after consummation of the Second Merger and shall hold office until their respective successors are duly elected and qualify, or their earlier death, resignation or removal. 4

ARTICLE II

MERGER CONSIDERATION

2.1. Delivery of Evidence of TRM Common Stock. As soon as reasonably practicable after the Effective Time, TRM shall deposit with its transfer agent evidence of book-entry shares representing TRM Common Stock issuable as Merger Consideration pursuant to Section 1.5(c).

2.2. Fractional Shares. Each holder of TRM Common Stock converted pursuant to the Merger that would otherwise have been entitled to receive a fraction of a share of TRM Common Stock pursuant to Section 1.5(c) shall receive, in lieu thereof, cash (without interest) in an amount equal to the product of (i) such fractional part of a share of TRM Common Stock multiplied by (ii) the volume-weighted average trading price of a share of TRM Common Stock on the Nasdaq Global Select Market (“Nasdaq”) for the five (5) consecutive Trading Days ending on the third (3rd) Trading Day preceding the Closing Date (as reported by Bloomberg L.P. or its successor or, if not reported thereon, another authoritative source selected by TRM that is reasonably acceptable to TRM). For purposes of this Section 2.2, all fractional shares to which a single record holder would be entitled shall be aggregated and calculations shall be rounded to three decimal places.

2.3. Paying and Exchange Agent. Prior to the Effective Time, TRM shall appoint TRM’s transfer agent or other bank or trust company to act as exchange agent (the “Paying and Exchange Agent”) hereunder, pursuant to an agreement in a form reasonably acceptable to each of TRM and TRM. Following the Effective Time, TRM shall deposit, or shall cause to be deposited, with the Paying and Exchange Agent cash sufficient to pay the aggregate cash for fractional shares in accordance with Section 2.2. Any cash deposited with the Paying and Exchange Agent shall hereinafter be referred to as the “Exchange Fund.”

2.4. Delivery of Merger Consideration.

(a) Each holder of record of shares of TRM Common Stock (other than the Cancelled Shares) in book-entry form that were converted into the right to receive the Merger Consideration pursuant to Section 1.5(c) and any cash in lieu of fractional shares of TRM Common Stock to be issued or paid in consideration therefor and any dividends and other distributions pursuant to Section 2.4(b), shall, promptly after the Effective Time, be entitled to receive the Merger Consideration, any cash in lieu of fractional shares of TRM Common Stock to be issued or paid in consideration therefor and any dividends or other distributions to which such holder is entitled pursuant to Section 2.4(b).

(b) Subject to the effect of applicable abandoned property, escheat or similar Laws, following the Effective Time, the record holder of shares (other than Cancelled Shares) of TRM Common Stock at the Effective Time shall be entitled to receive, without interest, the amount of dividends or other distributions which theretofore had become payable with respect to the whole shares of TRM Common Stock which the shares of TRM Common Stock have been converted into the right to receive. 5

________________

2.5. No Further Ownership Rights. All Merger Consideration paid by TRM in accordance with the terms of Article I and Article II shall be deemed to have been paid in full satisfaction of all rights pertaining to TRM Common Stock in respect of which such Merger Consideration was paid. From and after the Effective Time, the stock transfer books of TRM shall be closed, and there shall be no further transfers on the stock transfer books of TRM of the shares of TRM Common Stock that were issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time.

2.6. Net Asset Value Calculation.

(a) TRM shall deliver to TRM a calculation of the net asset value of TRM as of a date mutually agreed between TRM and TRM, such date to be no earlier than 48 hours (excluding Sundays and holidays) prior to the Effective Time (such agreed date, the “Determination Date”), calculated in good faith as of such date and based on the same assumptions and methodologies, and applying the same categories of adjustments to net asset value (except as may be mutually agreed by the parties), historically used by TRM in preparing the calculation of the net asset value per share of TRM Common Stock (with an accrual for any dividend declared by TRM and not yet paid) (the “Closing TRM Net Asset Value”); provided that TRM shall update the calculation of the Closing TRM Net Asset Value in the event that there is a material change to the Closing TRM Net Asset Value prior to the Closing (including any dividend declared after the Determination Date but prior to Closing) and as needed to ensure the Closing TRM Net Asset Value is determined within 48 hours (excluding Sundays and holidays) prior to the Effective Time; provided further that the TRM Board, including the TRM Special Committee, shall be required to approve, and TRM shall certify in writing to TRM, the calculation of the Closing TRM Net Asset Value.

(b) TRM shall deliver to TRM a calculation of the net asset value of TRM as of the Determination Date, calculated in good faith as of such date and based on the same assumptions and methodologies, and applying the same categories of adjustments to net asset value (except as may be mutually agreed by the parties), historically used by TRM in preparing the calculation of the net asset value per share of TRM Common Stock (with an accrual for any dividend declared by TRM and not yet paid) (the “Closing TRM Net Asset Value”); provided that TRM shall update the calculation of the Closing TRM Net Asset Value in the event there is a material change to the Closing TRM Net Asset Value prior to the Closing (including any dividend declared after the Determination Date but prior to Closing) and as needed to ensure the Closing TRM Net Asset Value is determined within 48 hours (excluding Sundays and holidays) prior to the Effective Time; provided further that the TRM Board, including the TRM Special Committee, shall be required to approve, and TRM shall certify in writing to TRM, the calculation of the Closing TRM Net Asset Value.

(c) In connection with preparing the calculations provided pursuant to this Section 2.6, each of TRM and TRM will use the portfolio valuation methods approved by the TRM Board or the TRM Board, as applicable, for valuing the securities and other assets of TRM or TRM, as applicable, as of September 30, 2020, unless otherwise agreed by each of the TRM Board and the TRM Board. 6

________________

(d) TRM agrees to give each of TRM and TRM and its respective Representatives, upon reasonable request, reasonable access to the individuals who have prepared each calculation provided pursuant to this Section 2.6 and to the information, books, records, work papers and back-up materials used or useful in preparing each such calculation, including any reports prepared by valuation agents, in order to assist such party with its review of such calculation so long as such individual remains employed by TRM or its Affiliates.

2.7. Termination of Exchange Fund. Any portion of the Exchange Fund that remains undistributed to stockholders of TRM as of the first anniversary of the Effective Time may be paid to TRM, upon TRM’s written demand to the Paying and Exchange Agent. In such event, any former stockholders of TRM who have not theretofore complied with any applicable requirements to receive cash in lieu of fractional shares of TRM Common Stock shall thereafter look only to TRM with respect to such cash in lieu of fractional shares, without any interest thereon. Notwithstanding the foregoing, none of TRM, TRM, the Surviving Company, Merger Sub, the Paying and Exchange Agent or any other Person shall be liable to any former holder of shares of TRM Common Stock for any amount delivered in good faith to a public official pursuant to applicable abandoned property, escheat or similar Laws.

2.8. Withholding Rights. TRM or the Paying and Exchange Agent, as applicable, shall be entitled to deduct and withhold from amounts payable pursuant to this Agreement to any holder of TRM Common Stock such amounts as it determines in good faith are required to be deducted and withheld with respect to the making of such payment under the Code, or under any provision of state, local or foreign Tax Law. To the extent that amounts are so withheld and paid over to the appropriate Governmental Entity, such withheld amounts shall be treated for all purposes of this Agreement as having been paid to the recipient.
